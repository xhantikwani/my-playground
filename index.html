> TOP 5 ANIME // FAVOURITES

#01

Attack on Titan (AOT)

LOOOOVE the whole shift from post apocalyptic survival story into a political war story where you could find yourself rooting for either side and feel mostly justified… I dont think anything’s come close to AOT storytelling wise ever Since it ended.

#02

The Great Pretender

Favourite anime to watch in dub. VA’s genuinely made me fall in love with the show. Its also very different to the average shounen- super powered slop I like :)

#03

Bleach

goated anime with goated soundtracks, everyone’s dripped out too…nuff said

#04

Death Parade

one of the the first anime I watched that wasn’t on TV… Really cool concept about the afterlife- it’s stuck with me ever since I saw it.

#05

Jujutsu Kaisen (JJK)

One of the best shounen I’ve read/watched. It takes a lot of elements from Bleach and other anime and managed to be its own thing. I think it’s stuck with me because I was there when it was releasing chapters every week. Reacting to leaks with oomfs used to so fun man
